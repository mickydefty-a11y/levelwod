# New Programs — Phase Overview (Batch 2)

Four new programs, each broken into phases first, following the same approach that worked well for the original three.

---

## 4. Conditioning/Engine Focus (6 weeks, 4 days/week, Intermediate)
Built entirely from Monostructural Conditioning — for someone who wants a stronger aerobic base rather than more strength or skill work.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Base pacing + technique | Easy-pace technique work across running, rowing, bike, assault bike; single unders |
| 2 | 3-4 | Building volume + jump rope skill | Longer steady pieces, interval work introduced, double under progression |
| 3 | 5-6 | Mixed-modal + testing | Combined conditioning pieces (e.g. row + run), benchmark test week (2k row, 5k run, bike test) |

---

## 5. Olympic Weightlifting Deep Dive (8 weeks, 4 days/week, Advanced)
For lifters who've already got Strength Focus's squat clean/snatch under their belt and want a purely technical deep dive.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Positional mastery | Slow, precise positional drills for snatch/clean, mobility-heavy |
| 2 | 3-5 | Progressive loading | Power → hang → full squat variations, building working weights |
| 3 | 6-8 | Heavy singles + jerks | Split jerk refinement, heavy single/double attempts, testing week |

*Note: given how technical this program is, I'll flag coach/video form-check reminders throughout, same as we did for the movement library entries.*

---

## 6. Strongman/Functional Focus (6 weeks, 3 days/week, Intermediate)
Built from Strongman/Odd Objects and Functional Movements — carries, sandbag work, sled work.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Technique across implements | Farmer carry, sandbag clean, sled push/pull technique |
| 2 | 3-4 | Loading progression | Heavier implements, yoke carry, atlas stone introduced |
| 3 | 5-6 | Combined medley + testing | Strongman-style medley (carry + carry + sled), test week |

---

## 7. Total Beginner On-Ramp (4 weeks, 3 days/week, Absolute Beginner)
Gentler and shorter than Beginner Foundations — for someone who has never exercised at all. Designed to feed directly into Beginner Foundations afterward.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Just moving | Walking, basic bodyweight patterns (no equipment), very short sessions (15-20 min) |
| 2 | 3-4 | First real structure | Slightly longer sessions, introduces the same movements Beginner Foundations Week 1 starts with, so the handoff is seamless |

---

## Build order
Given the scope, I'll build these one at a time, starting with **Total Beginner On-Ramp** (shortest, and sets up the handoff into your existing Beginner Foundations program), then Conditioning/Engine Focus, then Strongman/Functional Focus, then the Olympic Weightlifting Deep Dive last since it's the most technical.
