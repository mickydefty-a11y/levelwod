# Launch Programs — Phase Overview

Three programs for launch, each broken into phases (blocks of a few weeks) rather than written day-by-day yet — this lets us agree the overall shape before committing to full detail for 36+ individual days across three programs.

---

## 1. Beginner Foundations (12 weeks)
*Already started — Week 1 Day 1-3 built as a sample.*

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-3 | Movement foundations | Air Squat, hip hinge drills, incline push-up, scapular pull-up, easy conditioning pacing, ground-based core (hollow hold, L-sit tuck) |
| 2 | 4-6 | Building blocks | Goblet Squat, KB deadlift, ring rows, wall walks (first inversion work begins here), Russian KB swing, jump rope singles |
| 3 | 7-9 | First real skills | Empty-bar squat/deadlift/press, banded pull-ups, wall handstand holds, box step-ups, double under attempts |
| 4 | 10-12 | Consolidation | Loaded lifts at light working weight, negative pull-ups, box jumps (low), continuous running/rowing, retest week 1 benchmarks to show progress |

---

## 2. Strength Focus (8 weeks, Intermediate+)
For someone who's past the beginner stage and wants to focus on getting stronger on the barbell lifts.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Technique under load | Back Squat/Deadlift/Strict Press at empty-bar → light working weight, positional drills for Clean/Snatch |
| 2 | 3-5 | Progressive overload | Working sets across Back Squat, Front Squat, Deadlift, Strict Press, Push Press; Clean and Snatch technical work continues |
| 3 | 6-8 | Peak & test | Heavier working sets, introduce Push Jerk/Split Jerk, end with a testing week for new working maxes |

---

## 3. Gymnastics Skills Track (10 weeks, Intermediate+)
For someone who's got the basics down and wants to chase bodyweight skills — pull-ups, handstands, muscle-ups.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Introduces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Base strength check | Strict pull-up and dip strength targets, wall handstand holds, hollow/arch holds |
| 2 | 3-5 | Kipping mechanics | Kipping pull-up, kipping HSPU technique, toes-to-bar progressions, freestanding handstand attempts |
| 3 | 6-8 | Transition skills | Muscle-up transition drills (bar and ring), handstand walk attempts, chest-to-bar work |
| 4 | 9-10 | Putting it together | First muscle-up attempts, handstand walk for distance, retest strict numbers |

---

## Notes
- **Week 1 pacing fixed**: the sample day swapped the wall handstand hold for an L-sit tuck hold — inversions now first appear in Beginner Foundations Phase 2 (weeks 4-6) as a wall walk, with actual handstand holds not until Phase 3.
- Once you're happy with these three shapes, I'll build out full day-by-day detail one phase at a time (starting with Beginner Foundations Phase 1, weeks 2-3, since week 1 is already done) rather than all three programs at once — keeps each batch reviewable.
